Kindle Mahjongg
================

Unofficial Kindle-friendly Mahjongg solitaire game derived from GNOME Games
Mahjongg ideas and assets, with a Kindle-specific GTK2/Cairo interface.

Project lineage
---------------

Kindle Mahjongg is an unofficial Kindle-focused derivative/adaptation of
GNOME Games Mahjongg. Original GNOME Games / Mahjongg code, ideas, layouts,
and artwork remain credited to their upstream authors. Kindle porting
groundwork and packaging references are credited to GnomeGames4Kindle and its
author(s).

This package is not an official GNOME project or an official GnomeGames4Kindle
release.
